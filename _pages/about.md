<h1 class="main-heading">Hi there <img src="images/Hi.gif" width="40px"> Welcome to my Homepage!</h1>

I am **Mengke Zhang**, an undergraduate student in Internet of Things Engineering at the [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/).

My research interests lie in **multimodal learning, computer vision, generative models, and speech/audio understanding**, ranging from image restoration and depth estimation to audio LLMs, speech security, and autonomous-driving perception. I am currently a research intern at the [Medical Intelligence and Robotic Cognition (MIRoC) Lab](https://meweb.hku.hk/zljiang/), The University of Hong Kong.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in collaboration or potential opportunities.
